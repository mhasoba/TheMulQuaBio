# Extracted source assets

These files were extracted from the supplied 48-page *Analysis of algorithms*
PDF with `pdfimages -all`. Numbering follows extraction order. Soft masks are
retained separately because they are part of the original non-textual content.

The title slide credits the lecture as adapted from Robert Sedgewick and Kevin
Wayne at Princeton. Its 3-SUM example, empirical analysis, cost models, growth
classifications and memory material correspond closely to [Section 1.4 of
their *Algorithms* booksite](https://algs4.cs.princeton.edu/14analysis/) and the
accompanying [Princeton lecture
slides](https://algs4.cs.princeton.edu/lectures/keynote/14AnalysisOfAlgorithms.pdf).
This is the underlying source for the lecture structure and for at least some
of its visual material; Stanley's review should identify the precise origin of
each graphic considered for reuse.

The files are evidence for review, not a claim that MQB has permission to
republish every logo, quotation graphic, photograph or third-party diagram.
Nor does citation of Sedgewick and Wayne establish permission to republish
their slides or graphics.
Items used by the Quarto redraft are marked below. Text-heavy source graphics
were normally rebuilt as accessible text, code, equations or tables instead.
